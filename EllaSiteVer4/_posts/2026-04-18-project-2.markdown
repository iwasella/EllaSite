I focused heavily on Information Architecture for the initial prototype, making sure users would drill down from a national coastline view to specific local sites like Biloxi South without feeling overwhelmed by data. An interactive dashboard designed to visualize 50-year erosion projections, monitor, and identify 'High Priority' nesting habitats that require immediate conservation funding.
