goal: Redesign existing platforms for inclusivity for Codepath x AmazonNext Competition. My team proposed integrating an AI-powered freshness scanner into the Walmart App to make grocery shopping accessible for colorblind and low-vision users.
description: I was responsible for the Figma Prototyping, taking the existing Walmart Design System and seamlessly integrating a scanner that provides high-contrast, text-based, and audio feedback. We delivered a prototype that translates visual data into accessible feedback (e.g., "Banana is Overripe"). This solution follows Inclusive Design principles, benefiting not just colorblind users, but also elderly users or those with reading difficulties.
