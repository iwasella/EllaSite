goal: To create an immersive, emotionally-driven 2D platformer through original scoring and hand-crafted assets.
description: Developed as a solo project for my Video Game Design class, 'See You Soon' follows the narrative of a child searching for a friend that has gone to the great beyond. I served as the sole creator, handling every pillar of production-- composing original atmospheric scores in MuseScore, designing custom pixel-art sprites and tilemaps, and implementing core gameplay mechanics in Unity. The project served as a deep dive into cohesive world-building and technical game architecture and also as a testiment to blending my skills in art, music, and developing!
